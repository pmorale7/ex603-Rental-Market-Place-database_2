## Dashboard Team

# Rental Marketplace Findings

Dear Dashboard Team:
Our analysis of the Rental Marketplace data does not support the dashboard's findings of 50% of property viewings ended in failure.  Of the 200 viewings we reviewed, 156 were attended or 78%, 34 were cancelled or 17% and 10 were disputed or 5% in total.  Even if we put together both cancelled and disputed viewings as failures, the actual failure rate is only 22%, not the 50% claim by the Dashboard team.  We did identify an issue with missing values being excluded from the query results, which would mislead the dashboard findings.

Based on these findings, we recommend reconsidering the frozen quarterly payout.  Our data does not support the reported 50% failure rate, and low renter interest should not be automatically treated as a failed viewing.  the Dashboard team should correct its reporting logic and verify the results.  We recommend the quartely payout should be released.
