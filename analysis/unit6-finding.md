# Unit 6 Finding: Is the Platform Getting Better or Worse?

The Rental Marketplace shows some improvement in viewing failures over the period analyzed, but the improvement is not consistent. The monthly failure-rate analysis shows that the platform began January with a 23.81% failure rate and ended December at 18.75%. This indicates an improvement when comparing the beginning and end of the year.

However, the month-over-month results show significant volatility. The failure rate increased to 27.27% in March, fell to 0% in April, increased again to 25.00% in June, and later fell to 11.11% in October before rising to 18.75% in December. Therefore, the data does not support the conclusion that failures steadily improved throughout the year.

## Recommendation for Next Quarter

Leadership should focus on reducing the month-to-month volatility in cancelled viewings rather than relying only on the overall annual improvement. Months with large increases in failure rates should trigger a review of the properties and viewing events responsible for the increase.

The next-quarter review should monitor cancellation rates by property and investigate large increases as they occur. Previous analysis identified several high-failure properties, while Property_14 and Property_15 had no viewing activity at all. These groups require different responses: active properties with high cancellation rates need performance investigation, while properties with no activity need a review of their availability, pricing, listing visibility, and market interest.

The data identifies where and when performance problems occurred, but it does not establish their underlying causes. Leadership should combine these SQL findings with operational information about cancellations, property availability, renter behavior, and listing activity before deciding on corrective actions.
